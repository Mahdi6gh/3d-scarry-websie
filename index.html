The Legend of Unmasked

In 1969, four meddling kids and one extremely cowardly Great Dane named Biscuit drove a painted bus called The Clue Bus into a town with a ghost problem.

They solved it. Then they solved 311 more. Phantoms, swamp witches, space skeletons, a mummy with a mortgage.

Every single monster turned out to be someone in a costume. And the costumes were… honestly? Incredible. So the gang kept them.

Today we sell them — tailored, battle-tested, and guaranteed to make at least one Great Dane jump into somebody's arms.
